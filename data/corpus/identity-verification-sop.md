# Identity verification SOP (synthetic, restricted)

## Standard verification
Before discussing account details, the agent verifies two of: registered mobile number, date of birth, and the
last four digits of the registered payment method.

## Failed verification
After two failed attempts the contact is ended and a note is added to the case. The agent does not say which
answer was wrong.

## High-risk contacts
For contacts flagged high risk, the agent additionally requests a one-time passcode sent to the registered
mobile, and records verification code VER-9 in the case notes.

## Third parties
A third party may act on an account only with a registered power of attorney on file. Verbal consent from the
account holder is not sufficient.

## Retention
Verification outcomes are retained for 24 months and are visible to the fraud team only.
