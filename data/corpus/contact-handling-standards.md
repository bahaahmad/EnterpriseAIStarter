# Contact handling standards (synthetic)

## Answer targets
Calls are answered within 30 seconds. Chats are answered within 60 seconds. Email is answered within one
business day.

## Holds and transfers
A caller is not held for more than 2 minutes without a check-back. A transfer is always announced to the
receiving agent before the caller is moved (warm transfer).

## Handle time
The target average handle time is 6 minutes. Handle time is a guide, not a target to hit at the cost of a
resolved contact.

## Callbacks
If a contact cannot be resolved, a callback is booked within 24 hours and recorded in the case notes.

## Notes and wrap-up
Every contact is summarised in the case notes before the agent goes available again. Wrap-up time is capped at
90 seconds.
