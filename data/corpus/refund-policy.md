# Customer refund policy (synthetic)

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Services already consumed are refunded pro rata.

## Approval limits
Agents may approve refunds up to AED 500 without supervisor approval. Refunds above AED 500 require a team lead.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 7 business days.
