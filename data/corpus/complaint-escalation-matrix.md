# Complaint escalation matrix (synthetic)

## Levels
Level 1 is handled by the agent. Level 2 goes to the team lead. Level 3 goes to the customer relations
manager.

## When to escalate
Escalate to Level 2 when the customer asks for a supervisor, when a complaint is repeated within 30 days, or
when the requested compensation is above the agent limit. Escalate to Level 3 for regulatory complaints,
media mentions, or any threat of legal action.

## Timelines
Level 1 complaints are resolved within 2 business days. Level 2 within 5 business days. Level 3 within 10
business days, with a written response.

## Compensation
An agent may offer goodwill compensation up to AED 200. A team lead may approve up to AED 1,000. Anything
above that requires the customer relations manager.

## Record keeping
Every complaint is logged with a complaint reference in the format CMP-YYYY-NNNN.
